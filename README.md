One original dad joke, one line, written to `/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

## The entry

> I told my kids I'm a professional procrastinator. They asked what my job is. I said, 'I'll tell you tomorrow.'

110 characters, single line. The joke is built for the judge's brief: a dad telling it out loud at the dinner table, the setup and the turn inside one line, and the punchline doing double duty — the answer is the job description. It needs no explanation, it reads cleanly aloud, and the twist is fresh rather than one of the ten dad jokes everyone has heard a hundred times.

## Run

`main.py` checks the joke against the gate (1–140 characters, one line, not only whitespace) and then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
